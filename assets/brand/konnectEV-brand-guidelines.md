# konnectEV — Brand and Product System

## Strategic idea

konnectEV is the intelligent layer between driver intent and live infrastructure. The Mobility Weave represents those two routes as one coordinated experience.

The crossover matters: complexity is resolved without collision or dead ends. The symbol is not a road, cable, plug or circuit diagram. It is a proprietary mobility glyph designed to remain relevant as konnectEV grows beyond its initial charging-access product.

## Wordmark

The wordmark is intentionally independent from the symbol. `konnectEV` is one unified name with no separator. “konnect” remains a dense, confident unit and the coral “EV” signature provides the electric accent. Do not insert the symbol into the name or replace any letter with it.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Clear space: one terminal width on every side.
- Standalone symbol: minimum 20px digital or 6mm print.
- Horizontal signature: minimum 100px digital or 28mm print.
- Preserve the crossover order and negative-space underpass.

## Color

- Electric Coral `#FF3E1D`: the upper route, people, action and active movement.
- Electric Lime `#D4FF00`: the lower route, live infrastructure, availability and positive status.
- Deep Obsidian `#0D1117`: authority and primary app-icon ground.
- Warm Ceramic `#F3F0E8`: primary light ground.

The full-color logo uses Coral and Lime only. Electric Cyan `#00F0FF` may support telemetry and informational UI, but it must never appear in the logo, symbol, favicon, app icon, splash identity, or logo motion artwork. Monochrome artwork uses a deliberate underpass gap so both routes remain legible.

## Typography

- Space Grotesk: wordmark, headlines, data and high-impact brand language.
- Plus Jakarta Sans: body copy, product interfaces and wayfinding.

## Misuse

Do not add arrows, rotate, stretch, add effects, close either underpass, reverse the crossover order, alter the terminal shapes or assign colors outside the approved palette.

## Motion and feedback

- The motion mark animates each route along its own curve; it does not rotate like a generic spinner.
- Initial and full-screen loading may use the large mark with a short status sentence.
- Buttons and inline loading use the small mark. Skeletons use a quiet directional sweep.
- Charging progress moves from Finding → Joining → Waiting → Starting → Charging → Complete.
- Lime communicates connected, available, synchronized and complete states. Coral communicates active action and errors that need attention.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; the symbol remains visible without animated strokes.

## App and portal artwork

- iOS: Coral and Lime Mobility Weave on Deep Obsidian, no transparency.
- Android: separate woven foreground with Deep Obsidian adaptive background.
- Keep the full symbol within the central 66% app-icon safe area.
- Favicon: use the supplied simplified Coral and Lime artwork.
- Driver UI uses more expressive Coral action surfaces and Lime live status.
- Operator UI uses Obsidian navigation, Ceramic work surfaces, Coral attention states and Lime connection status.
